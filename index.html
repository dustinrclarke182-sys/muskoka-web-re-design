How booking works

  1. Pick a cottage and dates

    Compare the cottages above by size.

  2. Send a reservation request

    Fill in the short form on the resort's booking service.

  3. Pay the deposit

    A 50% deposit of your total stay is required when booking.

Cancelling? Give at least one month's notice before your arrival date, or the deposit is not returned.
