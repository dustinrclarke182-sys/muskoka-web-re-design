# Heart of Muskoka – homepage redesign concept

> **Independent, unofficial redesign concept.** Made for a design portfolio as part of the Muskoka Website Redesign Lab. Not commissioned by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Heart of Muskoka Cottage Resort. The real website is <https://www.heartofmuskoka.com/>.

## How to view it

No build step and no dependencies. Open `index.html` in any browser.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The homepage content and structure |
| `css/styles.css` | All styles, written mobile first (phone styles first, wider screens in `@media` blocks at the bottom) |
| `js/menu.js` | The phone dropdown menu (about 50 lines, no libraries) |

## Where the content comes from

All business facts were checked against the live site on 2026-11-06 (homepage, `/availability`, `/policy`): address, 7 cottages and bedroom counts, amenities, check-in/out times, 50% deposit, one-month cancellation notice, pets on leash, booking form URL.

## Open questions for the owner (not resolved in this concept)

- **Prices:** the live site publishes no rates. Every price here is a clearly labelled placeholder (`$XXX`).
- **Contact details:** the live site shows two phone numbers and two emails. This concept uses the ones in the live site's footer (416-991-5453, vacation@heartofmuskoka.com) until confirmed.
- **Cabin count:** the homepage says 7 cottages, the Our Property page says eight. This concept follows the 7 named cottages.
- **Evergreen:** labelled 2 bedrooms, but its page address says 3 bedrooms. This concept shows 2.
- **Photos:** placeholders only, until licensed photos are available.
